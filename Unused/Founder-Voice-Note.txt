Founder Module or Platform

What is Seedicon ?
Seedicon is an investment banking advisory firm that empowers startups and businesses with fundraising, strategic advisory, and investor engagement services.

What problem does it solve?
By connecting founders with the right investors and providing end-to-end investment support, Seedicon helps businesses accelerate growth while enabling investors to discover high-potential opportunities more efficiently.
User Types
- Founders (Specifically those are below series funding stage)
- Investors (Who wants to invest in startups that can make them money)

Flow 1: Explore the Platform
Landing Page → Sign Up → Select User Type (Founder) → Choose "Want to Explore" → Complete Basic Founder Onboarding → Dashboard

Flow 2: List Startup
Landing Page → Sign Up → Select User Type (Founder) → Choose "Want to List Startup" → Complete Product Listing Form → Pay $5 Listing Fee → Submit for Admin Verification → Product Listed → Product Listing Page

Flow 3: Raise Funds
Landing Page → Sign Up → Select User Type (Founder) → Choose "Want to Raise Funds" → Complete Fundraising Form → Submit to Seedicon (Investor Curation & Matching by seedicon )→ Dashboard

Business Rules
Platform Access
Only registered users can access the Founder Dashboard.
User can add few details while onboarding that are mandatory and use our platform
After addition of basic details we will verify until that verification they can’t message anyone of the founder inside the platform.
While onboarding we can mention phone number as mandatory so that fake profiles are reduced. OR Allow signing up with google and make sure that disposable mails are not used.
Users must select a role (Founder or Investor) during signup.
Duplication of user needs to be handled
Startup Listing
Only founders can create and manage startup listings.
A $5 listing fee is mandatory before submission.
Listings remain Pending Review until approved by the Seedicon team.
Only approved listings are publicly visible.
Founders can edit Draft or Rejected listings and resubmit them.
A startup can have only one active public listing at a time.
Fundraising
Only founders can submit fundraising applications.
Fundraising requests are manually reviewed by the Seedicon team.
Submission does not guarantee investor meetings or funding.
Founder Network
Only founders can access the Founder Network.
Founders can message only other founders within the platform. If they are verifed
Users can report or block inappropriate accounts.
Spam, solicitation, and abusive content are prohibited.
Government Grants
Grant information is for discovery purposes only.
Applications are redirected to the official government portal.
Seedicon does not guarantee grant approval.
Real Market Problems
Problems are AI-generated or aggregated from public sources.
Content is informational and may not represent real-time market conditions.
Founder Challenges
Posts can be published anonymously or with the founder's identity.
Founders can comment, react, and share solutions.
Seedicon reserves the right to moderate or remove inappropriate content.
Pitch Deck Reviewer
Only PDF, PPT, and PPTX files are supported.
AI analysis starts automatically after successful upload.
Uploaded decks remain private to the founder.
AI recommendations are advisory and do not guarantee fundraising success.
Users can regenerate the analysis.
Users can rebuild their deck using Deckwale directly from the review.
Due Diligence Checklist
Checklists are displayed based on the startup's selected industry.
Content is intended as guidance and may change over time.
Completion of the checklist does not guarantee investment readiness.
Startup News & Blogs
News is aggregated from public sources.
Blog content is published and managed by Seedicon.
External content links redirect users to their original source.
Product Analytics
Analytics are available only for the founder who owns the listing.
Metrics are updated periodically and may not be real-time.
Public users cannot access another startup's analytics.
Dashboard
Dashboard content is personalized based on the founder's profile.
Recommendations, grants, events, and articles are dynamically updated.
Some recommendations may be AI-generated.
Admin Permissions
Admins can Approve, Reject, Request Changes, or Remove startup listings.
Admins can moderate posts, comments, and reported content.
Admins can manage grants, blogs, featured startups, and platform announcements.
Notifications
Users receive notifications for listing status changes, fundraising updates, messages, and important platform activities.
Security & Privacy
Only public startup information is visible to other users.
Uploaded pitch decks and fundraising documents remain private unless explicitly shared.
User data is handled according to the platform's Privacy Policy.
Message
File or image attachments are not allowed.
Only verified one can chat
