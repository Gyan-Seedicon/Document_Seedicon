How Investors Approach LPs for Investment

The GP defines a fund thesis, brings LPs onto the platform, and converts their agreement into committed, called capital — through either of two capital call paths.

GP / InvestorLPSystem
1
GP creates a FundGP
Investor sets up a new fund entity on the platform and defines the fund thesis and target fund size.
2
Thesis shared with LPs on the platformGP
The fund thesis is published to prospective LPs through the platform.
3
LP receives & onboardsLP
If the LP is new to the platform, they create an account before they can view anything shared with them.
4
LP reviews the thesisLP
LP reads and evaluates the fund thesis to decide whether to commit capital.
5
LP marked as ApprovedSystem
If the LP agrees, their status on the GP's LP list updates to Approved — this is what unlocks the ability to commit capital.
6
LP commits capitalLP
The commitment is recorded and falls under Capital Calls for tracking.

GP-Initiated Capital Call

01Investor manually creates a capital call.
02GP adds all relevant LPs to the call.
03LPs receive it and accept.

Fund raises capitalSystem
Accepted capital calls (from either path) accumulate toward the fund's target size.
8
GP deploys capital into startupsGP
Raised capital is invested into the shortlisted / finalized startups — continued in Flow 02.

How Startups Get Shortlisted

Documented for the new fund path first. Worked example: a fresh ₹20 Cr fund, sourcing startups inbound through a public submission link.

GP / InvestorFounderSystem
1
Investor onboards on the platformGP
Starting point of the worked example.
2

A new fund is created with the target corpus.
3
Creates & shares the thesisGP
Investor writes the thesis and shares it with co-investors / LPs so everyone aligns on one page.
4
Thesis reviewed & approvedLP
Co-investors / LPs review and approve before sourcing begins.
5
Public submission link createdGP
Investor sources startups by generating a public submission link for founders to respond to.
6
Link shared for inbound responseGP
The submission link is distributed to founders to collect inbound applications.
7
Founder fills & submitsFounder
Founder completes the form and sends it to the investor.
8

Every startup that submits the form lands in the platform's Startups Pool.
9
Investor marks startups as PotentialGP
From the pool, the investor flags the ones worth pursuing. These get added to the Deal CRM as Potential.
10
Meetings & review cycleGP
Investor and founder go through multiple meetings and reviews; the Deal CRM stage updates as the relationship progresses.
11
Deal CRM moves to Due DiligenceSystem
Once a startup is selected to move forward, its Deal CRM stage changes to Due Diligence — see Flow 03.
12
Added to PortfolioGP
Once DD and the term sheet are complete, the investor adds the startup to the Portfolio — investment is now live. See Flow 06.
Note: this is documented for the new-fund / public-link sourcing path only. Additional sourcing flows (referrals, warm intros, scouts) will be layered in later.
