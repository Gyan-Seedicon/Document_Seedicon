Introduction

This document defines the purpose, scope, and requirements of the Investor Module on Seedicon. It is intended to guide product, design, and engineering teams through a shared understanding of what needs to be built and why.

PurposeTo describe the investor facing experience on Seedicon in enough detail that design, engineering, and QA teams can plan, build, and validate the module with a shared understanding.
ScopeInvestor onboarding, dashboard, funds, public submission links, document storage, investment thesis, LP lists, startup pool, pipeline, data room, reporting, founder reports, team management, DD partners, history logs, and the related admin oversight modules.
Out of ScopeFounder side workflows beyond what is required for interaction with investors, billing and payment processing, and platform wide mobile application specifications. These are expected to be addressed in separate documents.
AssumptionsInvestors have completed account creation and email verification before reaching the onboarding flow described in this document. Team and permission structures build on a shared role based access model used across the platform.
03

Product Overview

Seedicon is an investment banking advisory platform that helps startups raise capital and helps investors discover and manage promising opportunities, all within one connected system.

Founders below the Series funding stage often struggle to reach the right investors, while investors spend significant time sourcing, evaluating, and managing deals across disconnected tools such as email, spreadsheets, and generic file storage. Seedicon addresses both sides of this gap by connecting founders with relevant investors and giving investors a purpose built workspace to manage the full investing lifecycle, from first contact through due diligence and ongoing reporting.

Platform TypeInvestment banking advisory and deal management platform
Primary Value PropositionConnects founders with the right investors and gives investors a single workspace to source, evaluate, and manage deals
Core Problem SolvedFragmented, manual investor discovery and deal management that slows down both fundraising and investing
Module Covered in this DocumentInvestor Module, including the related sections of the Admin Portal

User Types

Seedicon serves two primary user types. This document focuses on the Investor experience.

User TypeDescription
FoundersEarly stage founders, specifically those below the Series funding stage, who are seeking capital and advisory support to grow their business.
InvestorsIndividuals or firms who want to invest in startups that can generate strong returns. This includes Angel Investors, Venture Capitalists, Micro VCs, Family Offices, Syndicate Leads, Corporate VCs, and Accelerators or Incubators.

Feature Details

Full description and key capabilities for each feature listed in Section 07.

F01

Dashboard

The Dashboard is the investor's home base within Seedicon, presenting a consolidated view of activity across the platform as soon as they sign in. It brings together startup visibility, pipeline health, and recent inbound activity so investors do not need to navigate multiple sections to stay informed.

  • Snapshot list of tracked startups with core details
  • Key metrics such as startups reviewed, active pipeline count, and documents shared
  • Deal pipeline summary broken down by stage
  • Recent inbound startups received through the public submission link
F02

Funds

The Funds section allows investors, particularly those managing capital across multiple vehicles, to organize their investing activity by fund. Each fund can be added from the sidebar and tracked independently.

  • Add and label multiple funds
  • Track amounts allocated or charged to each fund
  • Associate pipeline startups and reports with a specific fund
  • View fund level summaries alongside the overall dashboard
F03

Public Submission Link

The Public Submission Link gives investors a simple way to source startups without manual coordination. Investors generate a shareable link that opens a due diligence intake form, which they can tailor to the exact information they want to collect.

  • Generate a unique public link per investor or fund
  • Add or remove fields on the intake form
  • Automatic routing of completed submissions into the Startup Pool
  • Track submission volume over time
F04

All Documents

All Documents functions as the investor's private document library within Seedicon, removing the need for a separate storage tool. Beyond storage, it surfaces how recipients engage with shared files.

  • Upload, organize, and retrieve documents in folders
  • Track total views and average time spent per document
  • Visual engagement graphs for shared materials
  • Centralized access across the investor account
F05

Investment Thesis

Investment Thesis lets investors formalize what they are looking for, defining target industries, stages, and startup types. Multiple theses can be maintained to reflect different funds or strategies, and existing thesis documents can be imported directly.

  • Create multiple theses with defined criteria
  • Import an existing thesis file from a local device
  • Use thesis criteria to guide filtering in the Startup Pool
  • Edit or archive theses as strategy evolves
F06

LP Lists

LP Lists centralizes everything related to an investor's Limited Partners, keeping records and reporting history in a single organized place rather than spread across spreadsheets and email threads.

  • Maintain LP contact and commitment records
  • Link LPs to relevant funds
  • Track report history sent to each LP
  • Keep LP information private and isolated by default
F07

Startup Pool

The Startup Pool is the landing area for every inbound startup received through an investor's submission link. From here, investors can review full startup profiles and filter the pool to focus on the opportunities that match their criteria.

  • View all inbound startups in a single list
  • Open a detailed profile for each startup, showing submitted form responses
  • Filter startups by industry, stage, or custom criteria
  • Move a startup into the pipeline directly from the pool
F08

Pipeline Startups

Pipeline Startups works as a save or bookmark space where investors track opportunities they want to pursue further. Startups can be grouped into folders that reflect a thesis, a fund, or a stage of evaluation.

  • Add startups from the pool into the pipeline
  • Create custom folders for organization
  • Move startups between folders as evaluation progresses
  • Restrict pipeline additions to authorized investor or team members
F09

Data Room

The Data Room functions as a Virtual Data Room, giving investors a secure space to manage due diligence documents for each deal. Investors can structure a VDR into folders such as General, Financial, and Legal, and share it with founders or other stakeholders under controlled permissions. Where a founder has already shared their own VDR with the investor, it also appears here.

  • Create multiple VDRs, one per deal or relationship
  • Organize documents into General, Financial, and Legal folders
  • Add notes and manage sharing permissions per recipient
  • View analytics and an activity log for every VDR
  • Access VDRs shared by founders
F10

Reporting

Reporting supports investors and VCs in preparing and submitting performance updates to their LPs, with a complete history of what was sent and when, maintained inside the platform for future reference.

  • Build and submit performance reports to LPs
  • Maintain a record of past reports and submission dates
  • Track report status such as draft, submitted, or acknowledged
  • Reuse report templates across reporting cycles
F11

Founder Reports

Founder Reports lets investors request periodic updates from the founders in their portfolio and manage the full lifecycle of those requests from one place.

  • Send report requests to individual founders
  • Track which requests are pending, submitted, or overdue
  • Store received founder reports alongside other documents
  • Set recurring reporting schedules where needed
F12

My Team

My Team allows investors to bring colleagues onto the platform and control what each person can see and do, particularly around access to VDRs and other sensitive features.

  • Invite and manage team members
  • Assign permissions at a feature and VDR level
  • Adjust or remove access as team roles change
  • Review team activity through History Logs
F13

DD Partners

DD Partners covers external due diligence collaborators, such as legal or financial advisors, whom investors can add and grant access to specific VDRs whenever a deal requires outside review.

  • Add and manage due diligence partner profiles
  • Share selected VDRs with a partner without exposing unrelated data
  • Revoke partner access once due diligence concludes
  • Track partner activity within shared VDRs
F14

History Logs

History Logs provides a complete, auditable record of activity across the investor account, supporting transparency, accountability, and troubleshooting.

  • Record all critical actions taken across the account
  • Capture document level activity such as views, shares, downloads, and deletions
  • Filter logs by date, action type, or team member
  • Retain history for audit purposes, even after resources are revoked or deleted

Business Rules

Rules that govern how the Investor Module behaves, independent of any single feature.

RefBusiness Rule
BR01Investors must complete basic onboarding before accessing investor features.
BR02Investor data and documents must remain private unless explicitly shared.
BR03Investors can manage multiple funds, investment theses, LPs, team members, and DD partners.
BR04Access to sensitive data must be controlled through role based permissions.
BR05Startups received through submission links must automatically enter the investor's Startup Pool.
BR06Investors can customize submission requirements before sharing the public submission link.
BR07Startups can only be added to the pipeline by authorized investor or team members.
BR08VDR access must always be permission based and revocable.
BR09Every document that is shared, viewed, downloaded, edited, or deleted must be tracked.
BR10Team members and DD partners can access only the resources explicitly permitted to them.
BR11LP and founder reports must maintain submission status, history, and records.
BR12All critical investor activities must be captured in History Logs for auditability.
BR13Deleted or revoked resources should no longer be accessible to previously authorized users.
BR14Investor level data should be isolated from other investors unless explicitly shared.
BR15Investors must be verified by the admin team before their public submission link can be activated and shared.
BR16Investors and team members should receive notifications for key events, including new startup submissions, document access, and pending report requests.
BR17Archiving a fund, thesis, or VDR should not permanently remove the underlying data unless the investor explicitly confirms deletion.
