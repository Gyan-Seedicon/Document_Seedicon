Junify

Posted on August 27, 2026

Multi-tenant e-commerce SaaS platform for launching scalable online stores with automated Stripe checkout.

SaaS E-Commerce Online Shopping B2B Marketplaces

Detailed Description

Junify is an enterprise-grade multi-tenant e-commerce platform built for high-growth merchants and digital creators. It enables users to deploy custom-branded digital storefronts with sub-second page loads, automated Stripe Connect checkout flows, real-time inventory management, and multi-currency billing.

Engineered with an API-first serverless architecture, Junify scales horizontally to manage peak traffic surges during flash sales and product drops with 99.98% historical uptime.

Key Features & Specifications

  • Multi-Tenant Architecture: Instant isolation of client databases and custom domain routing per merchant store.
  • Automated Stripe Billing: Integrated subscriptions, one-time checkout, and automated sales tax calculation via Stripe Tax.
  • Real-Time Analytics & Webhooks: Event-driven webhooks for order fulfillment, inventory sync, and client CRM tracking.
  • White-Label Customization: Full theme engine allowing complete CSS, typography, and layout customization.
