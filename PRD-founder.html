2. Executive Summary

Seedicon is an investment banking advisory platform that helps early-stage founders raise funds, gain visibility, and connect with the right investors, while giving investors an efficient way to discover high-potential startups. The Founder Module is the founder-facing half of the platform: it covers onboarding, startup listing, fundraising submission, founder networking, and a set of supporting tools (grants, market research, pitch deck review, due diligence, and analytics) that help founders get investment-ready.

This document describes the problem being solved, the two primary user types, the core user journeys, the complete feature set, onboarding and listing forms, governing business rules, AI-driven automations, success metrics, and the pages required for design. It is meant to be the single reference point for what ships in the first version of the Founder Module.

2.1 Problem Statement

  • Problem: Early-stage founders struggle to get discovered by the right investors and lack structured guidance on fundraising readiness, while investors struggle to efficiently find high-potential startups.
  • Solution: Seedicon connects founders with curated investors and provides end-to-end fundraising support, startup visibility tools, and founder resources, all in one platform.
  • Outcome: Founders accelerate their growth and fundraising journey; investors discover vetted, high-potential opportunities more efficiently.

6.1 Detailed Feature Descriptions

Each feature below includes its purpose, key components, and governing business rules, so design and engineering can build against a shared understanding.

F01 Dashboard

The Dashboard is the founder's landing page after onboarding. It personalizes content based on the founder's profile to surface relevant news, events, grants, and readiness tools without requiring active search.

Key ComponentDetail
News FeedCurated startup news relevant to the founder's industry.
Upcoming EventsLocal startup events based on founder's city or country.
Grant AlertsRelevant government grant opportunities.
Learning BlogsSeedicon-published articles for founder education.
Due Diligence WidgetQuick-access checklist status for fundraising readiness.

Business Rules

  • Content is personalized based on founder profile data.
  • Grants, events, and recommendations update dynamically.
  • AI-generated content is clearly marked as such.

F02 Product Listing (Products Hub)

A Product Hunt-style discovery surface where approved startups are publicly listed. This is the primary visibility feature for founders who want their product discovered by the community and, indirectly, by investors.

Key ComponentDetail
Discovery GridBrowsable grid of listed products with filters (industry, stage, etc.).
Social EngagementUpvotes and engagement signals on each listing.
Product Detail ViewFull listing page per product with all submitted details.

Business Rules

  • Only founders can create and manage startup listings.
  • A mandatory $5 listing fee is required before submission.
  • Listings remain Pending Review until approved by Seedicon admins; only approved listings are publicly visible.
  • Founders can edit and resubmit Draft or Rejected listings.
  • A startup can have only one active public listing at a time, and duplicate listings are not allowed.
  • Featured Startup listings are selected exclusively by the Seedicon team.
  • Deleted listings can be restored by an admin within a defined retention period.

F03 Founder Network

A networking space exclusively for verified founders. Investors and recruiters are excluded by design, so founders can connect and discuss openly with peers.

Key ComponentDetail
Founder DirectorySearchable and filterable list of verified founder profiles.
Profile DetailsView of a founder's background, company, and experience.
Messaging Entry PointDirect link to start a conversation with another founder.

Business Rules

  • Access is restricted to verified founders only.
  • Unverified founders cannot send internal messages until basic profile verification is completed.

F04 Government Grants

A discovery directory of government grant opportunities, helping founders find non-dilutive funding sources relevant to their stage and geography.

Key ComponentDetail
Grant CardsSummary card per grant with key eligibility details.
FiltersFilter by geography, industry, or stage.
Apply RedirectDirect link out to the official application portal (for example, Grants of India).

Business Rules

  • Grant listings are for discovery purposes only.
  • Applications redirect to official government portals.
  • Seedicon does not guarantee grant approvals.

F05 Real Market Problems

A research database of real-world market problems, helping founders validate ideas or discover new opportunity areas grounded in real demand signals.

Key ComponentDetail
Problem FeedList of aggregated market problems with source context.
Category TagsTags to filter problems by industry or theme.

Business Rules

  • Problems are aggregated via AI or scraped from public sources.
  • Content is informational and may not represent real-time conditions.

F06 Founder Struggles and Challenges

A community forum where founders post building challenges, either publicly or anonymously, and receive advice and shared experiences from peers.

Key ComponentDetail
Post FeedChronological or trending feed of founder-submitted challenges.
Anonymous ToggleOption to post without revealing founder identity.
Comment SectionThreaded advice and reactions from other founders.

Business Rules

  • Posts can be published anonymously or under the founder's identity.
  • Founders can comment, react, and share solutions.
  • Seedicon reserves the right to moderate inappropriate content.

F07 Pitch Deck Reviewer

An AI-powered tool that analyzes an uploaded pitch deck and returns improvement insights, with a direct path to rebuild the deck professionally via Deckwale.

Key ComponentDetail
File DropzoneUpload interface supporting PDF, PPT, and PPTX.
AI Insights PanelStructured feedback generated automatically after upload.
Deckwale CTACall-to-action to rebuild the deck from scratch using Deckwale.

Business Rules

  • Supports PDF, PPT, and PPTX formats only.
  • AI analysis starts automatically upon upload.
  • Uploaded decks remain private to the founder.
  • AI recommendations are advisory and do not guarantee funding success.
  • Users can regenerate AI analysis or rebuild their deck using Deckwale.

F08 Due Diligence Checklist

Industry-specific checklists that help founders verify they have the legal, financial, and product documentation investors typically expect before a fundraising conversation.

Key ComponentDetail
Industry SelectorChoose the relevant industry to load the applicable checklist.
Document ChecklistList of required document categories with completion status.

Business Rules

  • Checklists are filtered by industry.
  • Content is guidance only and does not guarantee investment readiness.

F09 Startup News and Blogs

A learning and awareness surface combining aggregated startup news with original, SEO-optimized blog content published by Seedicon.

Key ComponentDetail
News FeedScraped headlines and summaries from public online sources.
SEO BlogsOriginal long-form articles authored by Seedicon.

Business Rules

  • News is scraped from public online sources.
  • Blogs are published by Seedicon for founder learning and SEO.
  • External news links redirect to original sources.

F10 Product Analytics

A private performance dashboard available to the founder who owns an approved listing, giving visibility into how their product is performing on the platform.

Key ComponentDetail
Views GraphTrend of listing page views over time.
Search ImpressionsHow often the listing appeared in search or discovery results.
Time SpentAverage time users spend on the listing page.

Business Rules

  • Available only to the founder who owns the listing.
  • Metrics are updated periodically.
  • Analytics are private and hidden from public users.
  • Analytics become available only after the listing is approved and published.

F11 Messages

A private, internal messaging system limited to verified founders, designed to keep conversations focused and free of spam or solicitation.

Key ComponentDetail
Chat ListList of ongoing conversations with other founders.
Message Thread PaneIndividual conversation view for sending and receiving messages.

Business Rules

  • Verified founders can message other verified founders within the platform.
  • File or image attachments are not allowed in messages.
  • Spam, solicitation, and abusive content are prohibited.
  • Users can report or block inappropriate accounts.
  • Guideline violations may result in account suspension or bans.

7. Onboarding and Forms

This section defines the structured data collected from founders across onboarding, product listing, and other submission forms. Field-level detail is provided so design and engineering can build accurate, validated forms.

7.1 Founder Onboarding Form

Basic founder details are collected once at sign-up. A LinkedIn URL can auto-fill much of this data via scraping (see Section 9, AI Integration).

Founder Details

  • LinkedIn Profile URL: Triggers auto-fill via Apify or equivalent scraping tool.
  • Profile Photo: Uploaded or auto-filled from LinkedIn.
  • Profile Banner: Uploaded image.
  • Full Name: Required.
  • Designation: Required.
  • Official Email: Required; used for verification.
  • Contact Number: Required; used to reduce fake accounts.
  • Country: Required.
  • City: Required.
  • Founder Background / Bio: Free text.

Professional Experience (Repeatable)

  • Company Name: Required per entry.
  • Designation / Role: Required per entry.
  • Start Date: Required per entry.
  • End Date: Optional if current role.

Education (Repeatable)

  • University / Institution Name: Required per entry.
  • Course / Major: Required per entry.
  • Start Date: Required per entry.
  • End Date: Optional if ongoing.

Co-Founders / Founding Team (Optional, Repeatable)

  • Co-Founder Full Name: Optional section.
  • Role: Optional.
  • Official Email: Optional.
  • LinkedIn Profile URL: Optional.
  • Add Member Action: Button to add additional co-founders.

7.2 Product Listing Form

The Product Listing Form is a nine-step guided flow. Step 9 enforces the minimum required fields before a listing can be submitted for review.

  • Step 1 — Product Description: Product Logo, Product Name, Short Description, Detailed Description.
  • Step 2 — Product Details: Website, Company Address, Social Links (LinkedIn, Instagram, X/Twitter), Key Features (multiple).
  • Step 3 — Product Media: Product Demo Video, Product Images (up to 6).
  • Step 4 — Product Integrations: Integrations (multiple).
  • Step 5 — Product Category: Industries (multi-select), Other Industry.
  • Step 6 — Target Market and Stage: Development Stage; FAQ set covering problem solved, target audience, uniqueness, business model, future plans, and usage.
  • Step 7 — Pricing and Competitors: Pricing Plans (name, price, features); Competitor Links (multiple).
  • Step 8 — Important Links: Link Name and Link URL (multiple).
  • Step 9 — Submit for Review: Final validation of required fields before submission.

Step 9: Required Fields Before Submission

The following fields must be complete before a founder can submit a listing for admin review:

  • Product Logo
  • Product Name
  • Short Description
  • Detailed Description
  • Website
  • Company Address
  • Industry (or Other Industry, if not listed)
  • Development Stage

7.3 Founder Struggle Form

  • Challenge Title: Required.
  • Description: Required.
  • Category: Required.
  • Tags: Auto-filled based on description via AI.
  • Posting Option: Anonymous or Named.

7.4 Pitch Deck Reviewer Form

  • Upload Pitch Deck: Accepts PDF, PPT, or PPTX formats only.

8. Business Rules

The rules below govern platform behavior across access control, listings, fundraising, networking, and content. They are grouped by functional area for traceability against features in Section 6.

8.1 Platform Access

  • Only registered users can access the Founder Dashboard.
  • Users must select a role (Founder or Investor) during sign-up.
  • Mandatory onboarding details must be completed before using the platform.
  • Unverified founders cannot send internal messages until basic profile verification is completed.
  • Phone number is required during onboarding to reduce fake accounts, or sign-up via Google OAuth with disposable email blocking.
  • User duplication must be prevented.

8.2 Startup Listing

  • Only founders can create and manage startup listings.
  • A mandatory $5 listing fee is required before submission.
  • Listings remain Pending Review until approved by Seedicon admins.
  • Only approved listings are publicly visible.
  • Founders can edit and resubmit Draft or Rejected listings.
  • A startup can have only one active public listing at a time.
  • Duplicate startup listings are not allowed.
  • Analytics become available only after the listing is approved and published.
  • Deleted listings can be restored by an admin within a defined retention period.
  • Featured Startup listings are selected exclusively by the Seedicon team.

8.3 Fundraising

  • Only founders can submit fundraising applications.
  • Requests are manually reviewed and curated by the Seedicon team.
  • Application submission does not guarantee investor meetings or funding.
  • Founders cannot submit a new fundraising request while one is currently under review.

8.4 Founder Network and Messaging

  • Access is restricted to verified founders only.

  • File or image attachments are not allowed in messages.

8.5 Government Grants

15.2 Layout Reference

Picture the screen in three horizontal bands and one vertical split, read left to right, top to bottom, the way a founder would actually scan it on login.

Across the very top runs a slim navigation bar spanning the full width of the screen: the Seedicon logo sits on the left as the home anchor, a global search bar occupies the center for finding anything on the platform, and the founder's profile avatar sits on the right as the entry point to account and settings.

Below that, the screen splits into two vertical columns that persist as the founder moves between features. The far-left strip is a narrow, icon-only launcher, the founder's fastest way to jump between Seedicon's family of products: Seedicon itself, Allbuddy, Deckwale, and Watch stacked at the top, with Profile and Settings anchored at the bottom of the same strip so account controls are always in reach.

Immediately to the right of that launcher sits a wider feature navigation sidebar, scoped entirely to Seedicon. This is where a founder spends most of their time choosing what to do next: Dashboard sits at the top as the default landing item, followed by Product Listing, Founder Network, Government Grants, Real Market Problems, Founder Struggles, Pitch Deck Reviewer, Due Diligence Checklist, Startup News and Blogs, Product Analytics, and Messages, in that order.

The remaining, largest portion of the screen is the main content area. Whatever the founder selects in the feature navigation sidebar renders here, so this is effectively a single canvas that swaps content as the founder moves between Dashboard, Product Listing, Messages, and every other feature, while the navigation bar, launcher, and sidebar stay fixed in place around it.

16. Assumptions, Dependencies, and Open Questions

The following items are called out for stakeholder alignment before or during development, as they are not fully specified in current discovery notes.

16.1 Assumptions

  • Deckwale and Allbuddy are separate, existing Seedicon-family products accessible via the tiny sidebar app launcher; this PRD does not define their internal functionality.
  • Payment processing for the $5 listing fee will use a standard third-party payment gateway; gateway selection is outside this document's scope.
  • Admin verification for onboarding and listings is a manual review process at launch, without automated approval.
  • Investor-side matching logic (how Seedicon curates and matches fundraising requests to investors) is defined in a separate Investor Module PRD.

16.2 Dependencies

  • LinkedIn auto-fill depends on a third-party scraping service (for example, Apify) and is subject to that service's availability and rate limits.
  • Pitch Deck Reviewer depends on an LLM provider integration for deck analysis.
  • Government Grants directory depends on maintaining an up-to-date mapping to official portals such as Grants of India.
  • Startup News aggregation depends on ongoing access to public news sources via scraping or a licensed feed.

16.3 Open Questions

  • What is the review turnaround time target for admin approval of listings and onboarding verification?
  • Should the $5 listing fee be refundable if a listing is rejected?
  • What retention period applies to deleted listings before permanent removal?
  • Is there a limit on how many active fundraising requests a founder can have historically (versus the one-at-a-time in-review rule)?
  • What specific criteria determine a Featured Startup selection by the Seedicon team?
