How Investors Approach LPs for Investment

The GP defines a fund thesis, brings LPs onto the platform, and converts their agreement into committed, called capital — through either of two capital call paths.

→
LP

→
LP

LP evaluates fund thesis to decide on capital commitment.
→
System

07

Portfolio Management & Reporting Loop

Dual reporting cycle: Founder reports to Investor, and Investor compiles & shares reports with LPs on schedule.

Leg A — Founder Reports to Investor
Trigger
Reporting Date Arrives
Auto-reminder sent to the founder's portal.
→
Founder
Adds the Report
Founder fills in the report in their portal and shares it.
→
Investor
Reviews the Report
Report is visible under Founder Reports; investor can approve or reject.
↺ On reject, the same loop repeats — founder revises and resubmits until approved.
Leg B — Investor Reports to LP
LP
Requests the Report
LP raises a report request to the GP from their portal.
→
Investor
Receives the Request
GP gets the LP's report request.
→
Investor
Compiles & Uploads
GP creates the report manually and uploads it to the portal.
→
LP
Approves or Denies
LP receives the report and responds accordingly.
↺ Ongoing cycle — repeats every reporting date across all portfolio startups.
